# Site principles

Hypertext Studio builds software for humans.

This website is the practical company home for Docket, LogDate, Curfew, company information, support, privacy information, and future product publication.

## Hypertext

Hypertext is information connected by links instead of forced into one sequence. For this site that means useful URLs, canonical pages, heading permalinks, feeds, citations, and paths readers can follow without an application shell. It is an information model, not retro decoration.

## Software

Today's products are purpose-built applications. The longer-term premise is that software will become more fluid: general interfaces will be reconfigured around a person's current work rather than every need living forever in a separate silo. The company does not need to explain that premise on every page; the products should be understandable on their own.

## Voice

- Say what the company or product does.
- Prefer concrete nouns and verbs.
- Do not invent a grand theory to introduce three applications.
- Do not claim novelty, inevitability, or moral importance.
- Keep the company description short enough to remain true.

## Design

- Minimal, readable, and intentionally proportioned.
- Current product information comes before decorative systems.
- ASCII artwork is real text, decorative, colored, non-selectable, and hidden from assistive technology.
- Light and dark themes follow the operating system.
- Focus states are clear without redundant borders or hard black.
- Playfulness comes from links, tags, addresses, and computing language—not clutter.
